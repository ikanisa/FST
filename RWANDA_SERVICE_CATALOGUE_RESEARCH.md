# FST Rwanda professional-services catalogue research

**Research date:** 26 August 2026
**Purpose:** Define a Rwanda-specific catalogue and defensible minimum professional fees in RWF. This is product and pricing research, not taxpayer, legal, lender or donor advice for a particular case.

## Conclusion

The Rwanda catalogue should not mirror Malta headings with country names changed. The most repeatable Rwanda demand sits in six connected operating systems:

1. RRA tax registration, VAT, EBM, WHT, PAYE, CIT/PIT, IQP, clearances and query response;
2. RSSB contribution schedules and unified PAYE/RSSB payroll declarations;
3. bookkeeping, bank/mobile-money reconciliation, payroll, close, management accounts and annual financial statements;
4. RDB/ORG registration and annual returns, corporate records, RGB NGO compliance and cooperative governance;
5. loan readiness, TCC/RSSB/CRB evidence, repayment modelling, BDF guarantee and BRD/DFI finance files;
6. donor/grant readiness, proposals, budgets, logframes/MEAL, post-award controls and donor reporting.

The implemented catalogue therefore publishes deliverable-based workpacks and packages, not generic promises such as “tax consulting” or “funding advice”.

## Authoritative Rwanda workflow findings

- RRA routes taxable businesses through income tax, VAT, PAYE, WHT and EBM obligations. VAT registration is compulsory when the RRA turnover tests are met, and RRA states that taxable activities must use EBM to issue tax invoices. VAT work therefore needs an EBM-to-ledger reconciliation, not only completion of a return annexure.
- RRA and RSSB provide a unified declaration for PAYE and RSSB contributions. The catalogue keeps PAYE and RSSB available separately but also publishes a combined payroll workpack and a monthly RRA/RSSB bundle.
- RSSB's mandatory pension rate is 12% of the relevant gross-salary base from January 2025, split equally between employee and employer, with a scheduled increase to 14% from January 2027. Rates are deliberately not hard-coded into public service descriptions because every workpack must use the rate applicable to its payroll period.
- RDB/ORG says online business registration is free and publishes post-registration and annual-return requirements. FST's RDB package is explicitly a professional preparation fee, not a resale of a free government service.
- RRA tax clearance and RSSB clearance are relevant to loan and tender readiness. The catalogue separates clearance readiness from the authority's certificate charge and taxpayer-controlled application.
- BRD programme material expects an application letter, a detailed business plan, multi-year income and cash-flow projections and supporting evidence. Loan support is therefore split into a diagnostic, application package, standardised bankability file, repayment/DSCR model, BDF route and BRD/DFI route.
- RGB's current NGO route requires governance documents, an annual report or action plan, a budget and source-of-funds evidence. The catalogue therefore includes RGB annual compliance, donor readiness, grant application, MEAL/budget, post-award controls and donor-reporting packages.

## Minimum-fee method

Minimum fees are FST commercial entry prices for the straightforward scope written on each card. They are not statutory tariffs and do not guarantee a final total.

**Commercial pricing decision — 26 August 2026:** the published Rwanda starting fees were set at 10% of the originally calibrated catalogue floors. The underlying scope, evidence and professional-review boundaries did not change.

Public Rwanda market checks showed:

- monthly payroll/bookkeeping offers beginning around RWF 50,000, with broader outsourced accounting reaching roughly RWF 349,000 per month;
- a visible Rwanda starter compliance package at RWF 150,000 per month covering VAT, PAYE, WHT, payroll and RSSB;
- publicly advertised bank/RDB-ready business plans in a range of roughly RWF 250,000 to RWF 600,000.

Those checks were used only as commercial comparators. FST's floors were then calibrated to the promised evidence, reconciliation and review depth:

| Work type | Published floor range |
| --- | ---: |
| Single recurring reconciliation or declaration | RWF 3,500-7,500 |
| Monthly small-entity finance/compliance package | RWF 15,000-25,000 |
| Annual tax, accounts, governance or readiness pack | RWF 10,000-60,000 |
| Business plan, loan file or grant package | RWF 50,000-85,000 |
| Feasibility or DFI/project-finance package | RWF 90,000-120,000 |

Commercial benchmark pages checked: [Live Accountants Rwanda](https://live.accountants.co.rw/), [Auxilia Consultants packages](https://auxiliaconsultant.com/packages/), and [BIX Rwanda business documents](https://bix.rw/bix-ai-services.html). Provider claims and credentials were not independently verified and are not used as legal or professional authority.

## Fee boundaries implemented

- Every Rwanda service has a non-zero minimum fee in RWF.
- Applicable VAT, official fees, notarial charges, lender/donor costs, translations, travel and other third-party costs are excluded unless stated.
- Transaction limits, employee limits, number of accounts, reporting periods and application count are stated where needed.
- Portal submission, tax payment, lender approval, grant award, audit opinion and regulated representation are not promised.
- Final written scope and fee remain mandatory before work starts.
