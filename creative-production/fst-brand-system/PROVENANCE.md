# FST Brand System Provenance

- Selected concept: Refined FST Wordmark.
- Requested refinement: replace the cobalt accent with FST orange.
- Approved source:
  `fst-selected-wordmark-orange.png`.
- Source production: OpenAI ImageGen, followed by deterministic palette
  separation and VTracer 0.6.12 vector tracing.
- Production colours: ink navy `#102635`, FST orange `#FF6845`, ivory
  `#FBFAF7`.
- The production SVGs retain the selected geometry and use paths rather than
  live fonts, keeping the identity consistent across browsers and devices.
- No KMFINCO logo, mark or visual asset is used.
