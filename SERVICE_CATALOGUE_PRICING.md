# FST service catalogue pricing basis

Reviewed: 29 July 2026
Market focus: Malta first, with EU and UK professional-services comparators where Malta firms do not publish detailed rate cards.

## Pricing position

The public catalogue uses indicative **From** fees for straightforward work with complete records and ordinary delivery timing. The target is approximately 50% below a comparable conventional full-service engagement, enabled by specialised professional AI agents handling structured research, first-pass drafting, reconciliation, evidence indexing and repeatable quality checks.

This is a pricing design target, not a promise that every FST fee will be 50% below every provider. The lowest automation-led competitors can already be cheaper in narrow categories. Statutory audit, legal work, CSP activity and other regulated outputs retain human professional time and cannot be reduced mechanically.

All public prices:

- are in EUR;
- exclude VAT, official charges, disbursements and third-party costs unless stated;
- remain subject to volume, complexity, quality of records, deadline and engagement acceptance;
- require a final scope and quote before work begins; and
- do not authorise FST to file or submit anything externally without recorded client approval.

## Public benchmark evidence

| Service area | Current public benchmark evidence | FST catalogue calibration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bookkeeping | Auriva publishes Malta packages at €180, €300, €500 and €800 per month. Papers publishes €159 per month. A4 publishes automation-led bookkeeping from €39 and accountant-reviewed bookkeeping from €79. Servox publishes bookkeeping from €120 per month. | Monthly bookkeeping starts at €100 for a small, clean ledger. This sits close to half of the €159–€180 full-service anchors while acknowledging that a narrow automation-only competitor can be lower. |
| VAT | A4 publishes VAT plans from €15 per month and quarterly filing from €35 per month. GSL publishes VAT-return work at €100–€350 per hour. | A straightforward reconciled VAT return starts at €45. |
| Payroll | PayTheStaff publishes €10 per employee per month; an Estonian EU comparator publishes €25 per employee per month for 2–5 employees. | Payroll starts at €10 per employee per month, with separate FSS support from €35 per month. |
| Annual accounts and company tax | A4 publishes annual financial statements from €300. A 2026 Malta company-cost guide estimates financial-statement preparation at €1,500–€5,000+ and tax-return preparation at €500–€2,000+ depending on structure. | Clean small-company financial statements start at €300 and a straightforward corporate tax return starts at €180. Complex files move to a scoped quote. |
| Statutory audit | A4 publishes statutory audit from €600 per year. | FST starts at €450 for a straightforward small-company audit. The reduction is deliberately less mechanical because independence, audit evidence, supervision and an authorised auditor’s report remain human professional responsibilities. |
| Internal audit and controls | A July 2026 ISO consultancy rate card publishes internal audit at £650 per day. A senior governance consultancy publishes internal-audit advisory at €150–€190 per hour and internal-controls design at €160–€200 per hour. | A focused internal-audit health check starts at €350 and an internal-control review starts at €600 per process. |
| Company formation and secretary | Published 2026 Malta guides place professional formation fees around €1,200–€4,000, with company secretary fees around €400–€1,000 per year. Official registry fees are separate. | Formation starts at €650 professional fee and company-secretary support at €250 per year, both subject to the required CSP permissions and excluding official charges. |
| Business plans and funding | Malta’s Business Reports scheme uses a €5,000 eligible consultancy-cost basis for a report or business plan, with a €4,000 fixed grant. | A full business plan starts at €900, a business-plan review at €250, and a routine grant application at €750. Complex institutional proposals start at €1,500. |
| Contract and legal-document drafting and review | EU comparators publish contract review from €65, basic contracts from €55–€80, privacy-policy drafting from €149, and commercial support around €90–€120 per hour. Other fixed-fee UK commercial drafting reaches £1,600 for complex transaction documents. | General legal-document drafting starts at €125, standard contract drafting at €150, review at €75, NDA drafting at €75, a service agreement at €175, privacy/cookie policy at €175 and a simple shareholders’ agreement at €400. Malta-law legal advice and reserved work remain subject to warranted professional review. |
